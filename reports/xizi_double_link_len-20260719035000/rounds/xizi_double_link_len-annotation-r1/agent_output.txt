An earlier candidate kept the cursor endpoint inside an opaque segment and QCP could not derive the condition-read permission. The repaired invariant completed three fresh qcp-mcp cycles with 2 auto and 5 manual witnesses, followed by canonical symexec and fixed case-lib compilation.
